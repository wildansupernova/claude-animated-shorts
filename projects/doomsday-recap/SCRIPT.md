## Facts (checked 2026-11-16)
- Avengers: Doomsday releases December 18, 2026 (US; UK December 16). It is a sequel to Avengers: Endgame and
  starts fourteen months after Thunderbolts*. Heroes from three universes meet Doctor Doom "amidst the collapse of
  the multiverse": Earth-616 (Avengers, Wakandans, New Avengers), Earth-828 (Fantastic Four), Earth-10005 (X-Men).
  Robert Downey Jr. plays Victor von Doom; Chris Evans returns as Steve Rogers; Chris Hemsworth (Thor), Patrick
  Stewart (Professor X), Ian McKellen (Magneto), James Marsden (Cyclops), Rebecca Romijn (Mystique), Alan Cumming
  (Nightcrawler), Kelsey Grammer (Beast), Channing Tatum (Gambit) are in the cast. (Wikipedia, Avengers: Doomsday)
- Watch-list consensus: Endgame, Loki S1-2, No Way Home, Multiverse of Madness, Quantumania, Deadpool & Wolverine,
  Thunderbolts*, Fantastic Four: First Steps, Spider-Man: Brand New Day; plus X-Men (2000), X2, The First
  Avenger, The Avengers, Infinity War, Wakanda Forever, The Marvels, Brave New World. (9to5marvel watch list;
  Disney+ "Countdown to Doomsday" collection, via search summary; Rotten Tomatoes / Deadline guides)

- Endgame (2019): time heist, Tony's snap and death, Steve stays in the past with Peggy; 2012 Loki escapes with the
  Tesseract. Loki (2021-23): TVA prunes branches; Sylvie kills He Who Remains; multiverse branches; Loki holds the
  branches at the end of time.

- Deadpool & Wolverine (2024): Earth-10005 is dying because its anchor, Logan, died; Deadpool recruits "the worst
  Wolverine"; the Void holds forgotten heroes incl. Gambit.

  the Avengers.
