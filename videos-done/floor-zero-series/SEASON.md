Six parts. Part 1 is posted and is fixed canon. Part 2 is rendered but not posted, so it can still change. Parts 3-6 are
new. It follows `references/INTERACTIVE_SERIES_FORMULA.md` and the viral skills in §10. The audit log is in §12.

**Full scripts:** `videos-done/floor-zero-part-2/SCRIPT.md` … `videos-done/floor-zero-part-6/SCRIPT.md`, with
line-by-line voice-over, gaps, picture per line, HUD and props.

## 0. The core idea
**Every rule is true, and it means exactly what it says.** The rules aren't lies. They're precise, and someone has
scratched parts of them out. Viewers win by reading the words. (This replaces Part 2's "the rules were there to keep
you" line. That line answered the season's question in episode 2 and made every later vote 80/20.)

Surface balance across the 6 votes:
- breaking wins in Part 1 (technically allowed);
- obeying wins in Part 3, and in Part 4 together with a loophole;
- Part 2 has no rule in play;
- Part 5 is a puzzle;
- Part 6 has no vote.

## 1. Logline (for the team; never spoken in full)
Seven floors under your office, one rule on each, and you have to clock out before 06:00. The one facing the wall only
moves when nobody is watching, and every step closer, it looks more like you.

Only the ≤ 10-word premise line in §7 is spoken. The "only moves when nobody is watching" key is never narrated; the
pictures teach it, so Parts 3-4 can't be gamed.

## 2. The truth (hidden; the finale pays it off)
1. **The Night Shift keeps one every Friday.** Part 1's phone reads Friday 23:59 (Fri 20 Nov 2026). Whoever hasn't
   clocked out on -7 by 06:00 is kept: drained grey and facing a wall, for good. There are hundreds of them by now.
2. **The touch.**
   - When it reaches you in the dark and touches you, it finishes copying you: face, voice, even your memories.
   - Then there are two of you. **Whoever leaves first leaves as you, and the one left behind is kept.**
   - In Part 2 (STAY IN) it touched you in the dark and simply left as you.
   - Once the game starts (Part 2's screens), the only way out is the clock on -7, and the clock takes one badge.
   - Part 4's grab is your *reflection's* hand, not its own, so no swap happens there.
3. **It only moves when nobody is watching it,** meaning in the dark or while you look away. Light is safety.
4. **The dark eats time.** After every blackout the HUD jumps forward on screen. The phone drains only while its light is
   on. That's why 6 hours pass in a few minutes of action.
5. **The closer it gets, the more it looks like you.**
   - It learns you from reflections (the elevator's steel, the water, dark screens), so its copy is mirrored.
     **Everything flips:** the badge (photo left, "YOU" right becomes photo right, "UOY"), your diagonal hair strand,
     and the wrist print.
   - Production: the copy is your character card rendered with `scale.x = -1`, so every asymmetry flips for free.
6. **The one in the elevator is last Friday's worker** (D. HALE · 14 NOV). Kept for a week, it waited on floor zero.
   - In Part 1 it got into the car during the jolt blackout (frames 1213-1230, 0:40), which is "someone you never saw get
     in".
   - The corner is visible and empty at 0:19-0:21, so it was *not* there all along.
   - It faces the wall to watch you in the steel.
7. **The building prints the rules; the kept ones scratch them out.** The canonical full card, shown only in Part 6:
   - "1. IF THE DOORS OPEN ON A FLOOR THAT IS NOT ON THE PANEL, DO NOT STEP OUT."
   - "2. IF SOMEONE IS FACING THE WALL, NEVER LOOK AWAY FROM THEM."
   - "3. THE NIGHT SHIFT ALWAYS KEEPS ONE."
   - **Rule 3's words appear nowhere else**, in no cold open, caption or line.
8. **Conventions:**
   - Photo plates carry the 06:00 date: "YOU · 21 NOV", "D. HALE · 14 NOV".
   - The badge says "YOU", never a name, from Part 3 on (it was unreadable before).

## 8. Episodes

### Part 2: "It Took a Step" (floor 0). Re-cut of the rendered episode; edits in §9.
- **Frame 1:** the car doors open. It stands one step past its own dusty footprints on the car floor.
  - Text: "IT TOOK A STEP. NOBODY SAW."
  - Line: "Seven floors under your office, and it just moved."
- **Recap:** "So, did you step out, or did you stay in?"
- **② STAY IN (loses; new sense: its voice):**
  1. The doors shut, and the panel dies.
  2. Without turning: "Don't worry. I'll finish your shift."
  3. "The lights go out. And in the dark, you hear it move."
  4. Lights on: you're the one facing the wall. "This is the end."
- **"Let's rewind."**
- **① STEP OUT:**
  1. 1 s insert: the panel with "0" lit, unnarrated.
  2. The car falls and crashes below. Relief.
  3. Every monitor: "WELCOME TO THE NIGHT SHIFT / 7 FLOORS · 1 RULE EACH / THE ONLY WAY OUT IS DOWN / CLOCK OUT
     BEFORE 06:00". The HUD starts.
  4. A ding: it's back, one step past its footprints. "And nobody saw it move."
  5. The stairwell door creaks open on the dark, with footsteps coming up.
- **Choice:** ① STAIRS / ② ELEVATOR (ride all the way down with it).
- **Last line:** "So, which way down do you go?"
- **Comment fuel:** "NEVER LO…": look at it, or look away?

### Part 3: "Don't Step in the Water" (0 → -1)
- **Frame 1:** you look down, scared; your reflection in the black water looks back up at you, smiling.
  - Text: "YOUR REFLECTION ISN'T COPYING YOU."
  - Line: "Floor minus one is flooded. Don't step in it."
- **Recap:** stairs or elevator, plus the real split.
- **① STAIRS (loses; new sense: touch):**
  1. Red dark. The footsteps stop on the landing just below.
  2. Your phone light finds the landing empty.
  3. The red lamp flickers, and a hand rests on your shoulder from behind. "This is the end."
- **"Let's rewind."**
- **② ELEVATOR:**
  1. "The doors close. Minus one is a long way down." The picture shows you watching it; no narration about the rule.
  2. Three light flickers. After each one it's a step closer, and the HUD jumps (05:59 → 05:20 → 05:02 → 04:47).
  3. In the brushed steel, your badge reads "UOY".
  4. "It's your height now. It has ears now. And it's wearing a red lanyard. Like yours."
  5. Doors open, and you back out.
- **Floor -1:**
  - Flooded, black-teal. "-1. DON'T STEP IN THE WATER."
  - The stairwell is on the far side.
  - At the far end the lights die, one row at a time, coming toward you. This is the Part 1 callback.
  - Unnarrated Easter eggs: a badge floating face-up, reading "D. HALE"; the reflection shows one extra person standing

- **Choice:**

  - ② CLIMB THE DESKS (slow; the dark is coming).
- **Last line:** "So, do you wade through it, or climb across the desks?"

### Part 4: "Never Turn Around" (-1 → -3)
- **Frame 1:** a wall of monitors showing you (the CCTV is in a high corner in front of you), and it right behind you.
  - Text: "IT'S RIGHT BEHIND YOU."
  - Line: "The rule on this floor: never turn around."
- **Recap:** wade or climb, plus the split.
- **① WADE (loses; new sense: it's in the water):** The water is warm. Your reflection doesn't copy your step; it looks
  up and pulls. "This is the end."
- **"Let's rewind."**
- **② CLIMB (3 beats):**
  1. The badge slips into the water.
  2. "The rule says step. Not reach." You reach in, and your reflection's hand grabs your right wrist.
  3. You tear free with the badge. Insert: a grey print on your right wrist.
  - The last row of lights dies as you reach the stairwell, and the HUD jumps to 03:12.
- **Stairs:** the unnarrated "-2" sign for 1 s.
- **Floor -3:**
  - Phosphor green. Every monitor shows live CCTV from a high corner in front of you: you, in this room, right now.
  - "-3. NEVER TURN AROUND."
  - On screen it stands one step behind you, just off your left shoulder, facing you, in a blue sweater. Its badge
    reads "UOY" and its hair strand runs the wrong way, visible to pausers.
- **Choice:**
  - ① TURN AROUND;
  - ② KEEP WALKING (with it at your back).
- **Last line:** "So, do you turn around, or keep walking?"
- **Comment fuel:** watch it on the screens? Does a camera count as looking?

### Part 5: "There Are Two of You" (-3 → -5 → -7)
- **Frame 1:** red dark, a punch clock at 05:57, and two of you, lanyards turned so no badge reads.
  - Text: "05:57. THERE ARE TWO OF YOU."
  - Line: "Only one of you gets to clock out."
- **Recap:** turn or walk, plus the split.
- **① TURN AROUND (loses; new sense: its breath on your neck):**
  1. Nothing behind you, but on the screens it's still there.
  2. You turn again. The monitors die one by one. "This is the end."
- **"Let's rewind."**
- **② KEEP WALKING:**
  1. Eyes on the monitors, row by row. It never moves while you watch.
  2. Callback slot: "Some of you said: watch the screens", only if real comments did.
  3. The screens die one by one, and the HUD jumps to 00:03:30. You dive through the stairwell door.
- **Walk down -5 (one image, sepia, one narrated line):**
  - Hundreds of photos, wall to wall, of people from behind, facing a wall. The camera lands on the empty frame, plate
    "YOU · 21 NOV".
  - The line: "One for every Friday. The newest frame is empty. It has your name on it."
  - Unnarrated: the frame before it, "D. HALE · 14 NOV" (a tall grey suit).
- **Floor -7 (red), four narrated beats.** The rows of kept ones are picture only.
  1. The card over the punch clock: "Only one of you can clock out."
  2. Your phone, still showing "No Service", lights up: Home, "OK. See you soon ❤". It's a reply to "Leaving now" from
     your number. "You never sent that."
  3. "05:57. And your phone's light dies." (Real phones kill the flashlight at low battery; the phone stays at 1 %.)
     In the dark, something takes your wrist. (It leaves no new mark: the wrist prints must stay the only two.)
  4. "When the light comes back, there are two of you. And both of you remember being you."
  - The shell game: in the **final** flicker, after the last swap, the lanyard of the figure that ends at ② spins and its
    badge reads "UOY" for 6-10 frames. That's the pause-to-find clue.
- **Choice:**
  - ① LEFT;
  - ② RIGHT.
  - "Look closely before you answer."
  - On the final hold, both badges face in. Two clues to argue about: the hair strands run opposite ways (readable on the
    bean heads), and the grey prints sit on opposite wrists.
  - Target split ~60/40.
- **Last line:** "So… which one is you?"

### Part 6: "Only One Clocks Out" (-7 → G, finale)
- **Frame 1:** gold morning through lobby glass, and one of you walking out, backlit to a silhouette so either branch
  could end this way.
  - Text: "06:00. ONLY ONE WALKS OUT."
  - Line: "You picked one of them. Let's see if it was you."
- **Recap:** the real split.
- **The causal rule for both branches:** the vote decides which one you never take your eyes off. It can't move while
  watched (Rule 2), so whoever is watched can't reach the clock first.
- **② RIGHT (loses):**
  1. "If you picked two, you kept your eyes on one. Nobody was watching two." It moves, and swipes its badge at 05:59.
  2. G, morning. It types a message home: "On my way." (The same words you type in the win.)
  3. At -7, one turns to the wall, and the blue drains out. "You were one. You spent the last minute watching
     yourself." "This is the end."
- **"Let's rewind."**
- **① LEFT (wins):**
  1. "If you picked one, then two is the copy. So you don't look away from it." Close-up: its badge, "UOY". Then the
     hair strand, the wrong way.
  2. "It learned you from reflections. The steel. The water. The screens. It got everything right. Except it learned
     you backwards."
  3. Without blinking, you back up to the clock and swipe at 05:59:59. At 06:00 it turns to the wall and goes grey.
  4. G, gold morning, one bar. Home: "Where are you??" You type: "On my way."
- **Final turn:** at -7, right after it goes grey, the unscratched card above the clock (§2.7; the car's card stays
  scratched). Rule 2 is finally readable, "NEVER LOOK AWAY FROM THEM", right after the viewer has watched it save you.
  Then Rule 3: "THE NIGHT SHIFT ALWAYS KEEPS ONE." Then G.
- **Epilogue:**
  1. Next Friday, 23:59. Someone else is the last one leaving. Behind them, the lights switch off, one row at a time.
  2. The car passes G, and with a jolt, the lights go out.
  3. When they come back, in the corner, facing the wall: someone in a grey sweater, red lanyard, the grey print on its
     left wrist. In the steel, its reflected badge reads "YOU".
- **Last line (word for word, Part 1's first line):** "Imagine you're the last one leaving the office."
- **Pinned comment:** "Watch Part 1 again. At 0:40 the lights go out. That's when it got in. At 1:20 it's standing
  in the corner. Season 2: would you warn them? ① YES / ② NO".
  - Both timestamps were checked on Part 1 stills: 0:40 is the jolt blackout (frames 1213-1230), and 1:20 (frame 2400)
    is the first shot with the figure in the corner.
