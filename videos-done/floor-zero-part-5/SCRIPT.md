## Rules for this script
- **Never narrate the key.** The keep-walking win shows that it never moves while you watch the screens; the
  narration doesn't say it.
- **Rule 3's words appear nowhere.** No "keeps one" anywhere in this part.
- **The -7 section has four narrated beats only:** the rule, the message, the light dying plus the wrist, and two of
  you. The rows of kept ones are picture only.
- **The copy** is your card with `scale.x = -1`, so the badge, hair strand and wrist print all mirror.
- **The -7 touch leaves no new mark.**
- **The "UOY" glimpse:** 6-10 frames in the final flicker, after the last swap, on the figure that ends at ②. On the
  final hold both badges face in; the clues are the hair strands and the wrist prints.
- **Cold open:** red dark, lanyards turned, no readable badge.
