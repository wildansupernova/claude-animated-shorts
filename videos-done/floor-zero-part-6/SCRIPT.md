## Rules for this script
- **The causal rule for both branches:** the vote decides which one you never take your eyes off. The watched one can't
  move (Rule 2), so the unwatched one reaches the clock first. The narration states only what happened. The full card
  then shows Rule 2 in words, right after the viewer has watched it save you.
- **Rule 3's text** ("THE NIGHT SHIFT ALWAYS KEEPS ONE") appears only on the full card, line 23, for the first and only
  time in the season.
- **The clue payoff** (badge "UOY", hair strand) gets close-ups. Explain the mirror in two lines, no more.
- **Cold open:** a backlit silhouette walking out, so either branch could end this way. No readable badge.

## On-screen text and props
- **Frame-1 text:** 06:00. ONLY ONE WALKS OUT.
- **HUD:** "FLOOR -7 · 00:02:31" → 00:00:01, then "FLOOR G". No HUD in the epilogue.
- **The full card (shown only here, above the clock on -7; the car's card stays scratched):**
  1. IF THE DOORS OPEN ON A FLOOR THAT IS NOT ON THE PANEL, DO NOT STEP OUT.
  2. IF SOMEONE IS FACING THE WALL, NEVER LOOK AWAY FROM THEM.
  3. THE NIGHT SHIFT ALWAYS KEEPS ONE.
- **Phone:** the copy types "On my way." in the losing branch; in the win you get "Where are you??" and type "On my way."
  The same words, typed by each of you.
- **The epilogue figure:** seen only from behind. The badge "YOU" shows only in its 2D steel-reflection insert.
- **Build notes:**
  - the twin uses `Place`'s `mirror` flag on ②'s front card;
  - the drained grey is a per-card desaturation uniform in `cardshade.ts`, not a per-frame recolour (the drawing
    pool is keyed by draw options);
  - the steel inserts are 2D;
  - the flashbacks reuse the rendered Parts 3-5, so render those first.
- **No end card:** the title "FLOOR ZERO" sits over the last corner shot, with no CTA in the video. The last frame loops into Part 1.
