# "It Rained Whale": script v1 (2026-11-15)
Format: ~70 s vertical short, viral formula: paradox hook → setting → escalation (warning ignored, countdown) → snap ("it rained whale") → "Wait...?" → "You see," + pudding analogy → ironic double payoff (Walter's car, "Whale of a Deal") → callback (Exploding Whale Memorial Park).

## Facts (sources)
- A ~45 ft (14 m) sperm whale, ~8 short tons, washed ashore at Florence, Oregon on 9 Nov 1970; detonated 12 Nov 1970, 3:45 pm. (Wikipedia "Exploding whale"; Mental Floss)
- The Oregon State Highway Division handled it (Oregon's beaches were legally a state highway, 1913 law); they treated it like removing a large rock/boulder. (Wikipedia; ABC/Sinclair "Amazing America")
- Plan: blast it into pieces small enough for scavengers (seagulls) to eat. (Wikipedia; Mental Floss)
- Walter Umenhofer, WWII veteran with explosives training, warned 20 sticks would be enough; the crew used about half a ton (≈20 cases). (Wikipedia; Audacy/Connecting Vets; Jalopnik)
- About 75 spectators; KATU-TV crew (reporter Paul Linnman, cameraman Doug Brazil) filmed it. (Mental Floss; Wikipedia)
- Blubber flew up to ~800 ft, hitting spectators, buildings and the parking lot. (Wikipedia)
- The blast scared the seagulls away; most of the whale stayed on the beach and was buried by the crew. (Mental Floss; Wikipedia)
- A slab of blubber crushed the roof of Umenhofer's new car, bought days earlier in a "whale of a deal" dealership promotion; the state reimbursed him. (Jalopnik; Mental Floss; Wikipedia)
- 2020: Florence residents voted to name a new park "Exploding Whale Memorial Park". (Wikipedia)
