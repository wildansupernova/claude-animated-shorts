# Floor Zero, Part 1: script

Episode 1 of our original interactive horror series **Floor Zero**, built on `references/INTERACTIVE_SERIES_FORMULA.md`.
Second person, present tense. It ends on a numbered choice that viewers answer in the comments.

- **Series pitch:** You're the last one leaving the office. The elevator goes right past the ground floor. Every floor
  below has one rule. Break it, and you stay. You have to survive 7 floors before 6 AM.
- **Part 1 choice:** ① STEP OUT / ② STAY IN.
- **Planned Part 2:** Staying in ends with the figure facing the wall saying, without turning around, "Don't worry.
  I'll finish your shift." Then "Let's rewind." Stepping out: the elevator falls behind you, and the monitor reads
  "Welcome to the Night Shift. 7 floors. 1 rule each. Reach G by 6:00."

## Cast
- **You:** our house bean-head office worker in a blue sweater with a lanyard and ID badge. Never named on screen;
  the monitor types the name as `YOU`.
- **The one facing the wall:** tall, grey suit, dark hair, seen only from behind. They breathe slowly and stand
  perfectly still. Their face is the season finale's reveal and is never shown before then.
